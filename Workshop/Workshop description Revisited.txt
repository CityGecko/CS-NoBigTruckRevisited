[h1]No Big Truck Revisited[/h1]
Big trucks don't deliver goods to small shops.

If you want more realism in which vehicles deliver goods to your shops, this mod helps: only small vans are sent to your small shops, so no more semi-trailers squeezing through narrow streets and making impossible turns.

Continuation of [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2069057130]No Big Truck[/url] by MacSergey, who no longer actively maintains mods due to time constraints. Updated for game version 1.21.2-f5, including the 1.20/1.21 fixes by Will.

[b]Please unsubscribe or disable all other versions of No Big Truck (original, beta, "Temporary Fix") - only one may be active.[/b]

[h2]How it works[/h2]
The mod looks at the type of the source and the target building of a goods delivery. If both are covered by one of your rules, only vehicles that are NOT marked as "Large Vehicle" in [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1548831935]Advanced Vehicle Options[/url] are used. So the small shop around the corner gets a van instead of a semi-trailer.

[b]Sources[/b]
[list]
[*]Industry
[*]Warehouse
[*]Outside connection (incl. cargo stations)
[/list]
[b]Targets[/b]
[list]
[*]Low Commercial
[*]High Commercial
[*]Eco Commercial
[*]Leisure Commercial
[*]Tourist Commercial
[*]Wall-to-wall Commercial (district specialization of the Plazas & Promenades DLC)
[/list]

The mod does not change savegames and can be enabled or disabled at any time.

[h2]Settings[/h2]
[list]
[*]Mark the trucks you consider large in Advanced Vehicle Options (option "Large Vehicle", shown for generic industry trucks with trailer).
[*]In the mod options, set up one or more rules: which source and target types, and optionally the maximum lot size (cells) of the target building.
[*]Every change is saved immediately and confirmed next to the rule ("Saved at ...").
[/list]

[h2]New in 2.0[/h2]

[h2]Support[/h2]
Please describe the problem in as much detail as possible and always attach your output_log.txt - reports without a log cannot be investigated. You find it in the game folder under Cities_Data\output_log.txt (upload it e.g. to pastebin or a cloud drive and post the link). The log contains a line "No Big Truck Revisited: Harmony patches: ... applied, ... failed" that helps a lot.
Bug reports are also welcome as [url=https://github.com/CityGecko/CS-NoBigTruckRevisited/issues]GitHub issue[/url].
